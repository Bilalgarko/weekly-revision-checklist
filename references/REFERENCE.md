# Reference guide

Details for producing a weekly revision checklist. Load this file only
when you need the specifics; SKILL.md has the main process.

## Priority scheme

Assign exactly one priority per revision unit.

- **High** - The topic has an exam, quiz or assessment within 7 days, is
  heavily weighted in the module, or the user rates their confidence as low.
- **Medium** - The topic matters for the exam but is further away, or the
  user has partial confidence.
- **Low** - The topic is light, already strong, or a nice-to-have if time
  allows.

When two topics tie, prefer the one with the earlier date, then the one
with lower user confidence.

## Estimating time needed

- A revision unit is roughly 25 to 60 minutes of focused review.
- Starting points: first-time review of a hard topic 50-60 min, second
  review 30-40 min, quick recall check 15-25 min.
- Add time if the topic needs problem practice (practice problems must be
  ungraded, not submitted work).
- Sum the estimates and compare with the hours the user has available. If
  the total is too large, move Low priority items to next week and tell
  the user.

## Full example

User says: "Help me revise. Second year biology, exam in 10 days, about
6 hours free, respiration and photosynthesis feel shaky."

Output:

| Topic | Priority | Time needed | Done? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cell respiration - glycolysis and Krebs | High | 50 min | [ ] |
| Cell respiration - electron transport chain | High | 50 min | [ ] |
| Photosynthesis - light reactions | High | 45 min | [ ] |
| Photosynthesis - Calvin cycle | Medium | 40 min | [ ] |
| Enzyme kinetics recap | Low | 25 min | [ ] |

Total: about 3 h 30 min of the 6 hours available.

## Edge cases

- **Vague request** - "help me revise" with no detail. Ask the short
  question list from SKILL.md, then proceed.
- **No dates known** - Order by the user's stated confidence, lowest
  first, and note the assumption.
- **More work than time** - Trim Low items first; say what was moved and
  why.
- **Request is really assessed work** - Decline that part per the do-not
  rules in SKILL.md and offer revision alternatives.
- **Mid-week check-in** - Regenerate the table with completed rows marked
  [x] and suggest what fits the remaining time.

## Practice quizzes

If the user asks to be quizzed, keep questions conceptual and answerable
from their own notes or lectures. Give feedback after they answer, then
reveal the answer. Never produce quiz content that could be pasted into
graded homework.
